{2025 Bahrain Grand Prix recap F1 results, highlights as Oscar Piastri wins for McLaren}
{ESPN}
{04-13-2025}
{McLaren's Oscar Piastri won the Bahrain Grand Prix from pole, claiming McLaren's first win in Sakhir.

Key points

Lando Norris was handed a five-second penalty for a false start and served it at his pitstop. He finished third after starting sixth, showing the pace of the McLaren.

George Russell jumped from third to second on Lap 1, behind Piastri

Ferrari's find their way into the top five with well-timed pitstops

A brief safety car comes out on Lap 32-35 after contact between Yuki Tsunoda and Carlos Sainz

Liam Lawson given two penalties for causing a collision.}
